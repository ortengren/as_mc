	Special Instructions:
	The ab initio benzene dimer interaction energies have been 
	calculated in the supermolecule approach at MP2/6-31G* level.
	The geometry of the dimer is specified by the three coordinates
	(X,Y,Z) of the center of mass and the three eulerian angles of molecule B 
	with respect of molecule A (fixed molecule). 
	The center of mass of molecule A is fixed in the origin (0,0,0), and the 
	benzene ring lies in the xz plane, being two C-H bonds on the z-axis.
